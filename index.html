How to read it. The stable estimate is the most common live guess over the trailing window — trust it over the live guess, which can jump around during intros, breakdowns, or key changes. Check the confidence tag next to the correlation bar: strong means the profile match is clear, while ambiguous or low signal means don't trust it yet. Press ▶ next to any candidate to hear its tonic triad and compare by ear against the song — the real giveaway is which chord the song actually resolves to, especially when the top two candidates are relative major/minor (they share every note, so the math alone can't always separate them). The chord grid on the left shows the seven diatonic chords of the stable estimate and lights up whichever one currently matches the audio best — if the highlight keeps landing on chords from that set as the song plays, that's good independent confirmation of the key. Works best on tonal, harmonically rich audio (full mixes, chords, sustained notes); solo drums or a single melodic line can mislead it. Give it 10–15 seconds on a new song before reading much into the result. Nothing recorded here leaves your browser.
